Maharana Pratap Airport sits in Dabok, about 22 kilometres east of Udaipur. It is a small airport and you are out of baggage claim quickly. The part that slows people down is outside: a line of drivers quoting fares that change with the hour, the season and how tired you look.

Aavre removes that step. Your car is booked and confirmed before you board, the fare is fixed at ₹499 for anywhere in the city, and your driver has your flight details. You walk out, your cab is there, and you are on your way.

Common questions

How much is a taxi from Udaipur airport to the city?

A flat ₹499 to any point within 35 km of the airport, which covers the whole of Udaipur city. The fare is agreed before you travel, with no surge and no night surcharge. Toll and parking, if any, are charged at actuals.

How far is Udaipur airport from the city?

Maharana Pratap Airport in Dabok is about 22 kilometres from the city centre, a drive of 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic.

Is the fare different at night or in peak season?

No. The airport fare is the same at any hour and any time of year. There is no night surcharge and no seasonal surge pricing.

Is the drop to the airport the same price as the pickup?

Yes. One way, pickup or drop, the fare is the same ₹499 within 35 km of the airport.

How do I book an airport taxi with Aavre?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94143 43817 with your flight number, date, drop point, number of guests and bags. We confirm the car and the fixed fare before you fly.

How much luggage can I bring?

Every car sits up to 4 guests. The BYD E6 takes 4 large suitcases, 2 cabin suitcases and a bunch of backpacks. The Tata Punch EV takes 2 medium suitcases, 1 cabin bag and a couple of backpacks. Tell us what you are carrying and we send the car that fits it.

Can you take us from the airport straight to Nathdwara or Kumbhalgarh?

Yes. Airport transfers to Nathdwara, Kumbhalgarh, Ranakpur and Chittorgarh run at fixed outstation fares, agreed before you travel. You can see them on our services page.
