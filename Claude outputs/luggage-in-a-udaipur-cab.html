How much luggage fits in a Udaipur cab

By Aavre    September 2026    6 min read

Almost every question we are asked about our cars is the same question, arriving sideways. Will our bags fit.

It is a fair thing to worry about. You land at Maharana Pratap Airport off a connection, there are four of you and five bags, and the car that turns up decides whether the first twenty minutes of the holiday are comfortable or spent with a suitcase across somebody's knees. Nobody wants to be negotiating boot space in a car park at eleven at night.

So here is the plain version. Every Aavre cab sits up to 4 guests. Both our models seat exactly the same number of people. The only thing that changes between them is how much luggage travels with you, which means the right car is chosen by your bags and not by your headcount.

Start with the bags, not the seats

Most people book a cab by counting passengers, and in a city of short hops that works fine. For a trip to Udaipur it does not, because almost everybody arrives by air or by train with a full set of checked luggage and then keeps it in the car for the next three days.

Four adults is four adults in either of our cars. Four adults with four checked suitcases is a completely different booking. That is the number worth telling us.

What actually goes in

The BYD E6

A full size electric MPV, and the reason we bought it. It takes four large suitcases, two cabin suitcases and a bunch of backpacks, with all four seats occupied. Nothing rides on a lap and nothing goes on the front seat. This is the car for an airport arrival with a family's worth of luggage, for outstation days, and for anybody who has more bags than people.

The Tata Punch EV

A compact electric SUV, and the better car for moving around the city. It takes two medium suitcases of up to 15 kg each, one cabin bag and a couple of backpacks. It sits up to 4 guests just as the BYD does. It is easier through the old city lanes near Jagdish Temple and simpler to park at Fateh Sagar, and for a sightseeing day with day bags rather than suitcases it is the one we would send.

A quick translation, because bag sizes are slippery. A large suitcase is a checked bag at the airline limit, roughly twenty eight inches. A cabin suitcase is what goes in the overhead locker. If you are unsure, count the bags you checked in at the airport and tell us that number. It is the one measurement everybody already knows.

Which car for which trip

Landing with a full set of bags

Ask for the BYD E6. An arrival is the one journey where every bag you brought is in the car at once, and it is also the first impression the city makes on you. Twenty minutes with a case wedged between your feet is a bad start to a good week.

A day of city sightseeing

Either car works, and the Punch EV is usually the nicer one. City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon ki Bari, Fateh Sagar and up to Sajjangarh for sunset is a hundred kilometres of short runs and long stops. You are carrying a camera and a water bottle, not suitcases, and the smaller car moves through the lanes more easily.

Kumbhalgarh, Ranakpur or Chittorgarh

The BYD E6 if you have the choice. Both cars complete these round trips comfortably and the range is worked out before the car leaves, so neither will strand you. But these are long days, well over two hundred kilometres with real climbs in them, and space matters more at hour eight than it does at hour one.

Nathdwara for darshan

Either. It is a short run with waiting time in the middle and most people travel light for it. Book whichever is free and spend the difference on prasad.

Wedding season, November to February

The BYD E6, almost always. Wedding luggage is not normal luggage. Outfits travel in garment bags that must not be folded, there is usually a box of something fragile, and guests are moving between a hotel and a venue several times across three days with all of it in the car. Tell us it is a wedding when you book and we will plan the cars around the outfits rather than the people.

Heading home

Count the shopping. Almost everybody leaves Udaipur heavier than they arrived, and a departure is the transfer people most often under book. Miniature paintings, a rug from Hathi Pol, marble from Nathdwara. Add a bag to whatever you came with and you will be about right.

The limits, stated plainly

Two things we would rather publish than have you discover at the kerb.

Both our cars sit up to 4 guests and neither seats more. Five or six people travelling together is two cars, not one, and we will say so when you book rather than squeeze you in and hope. Both cars are pre confirmed, both arrive together, and the group travels as one.

The Punch EV will not take four adults and four large suitcases. It is a good car and that is simply not what it is. If somebody quotes you a compact car for that load, they are planning to put a bag on somebody's knees and tell you it is fine when you are already sitting in it.

Just tell us what you are carrying

When you message us on WhatsApp, say how many of you there are and how many bags. Four adults and four checked suitcases. Two of us with cabin bags. A wedding, three days, garment bags. Any of those is enough for us to send the right car, and the fare is pre confirmed before you travel either way. Our published prices are on the services page.

There is no charge for asking and no charge for us getting it right. We would far rather send the bigger car than have you spend a Rajasthan afternoon with a suitcase on your knees.

Common questions

How much luggage fits in a Udaipur cab?

It depends which of our two cars you book. The BYD E6 takes four large suitcases, two cabin suitcases and a bunch of backpacks, with all four seats taken. The Tata Punch EV takes two medium suitcases of up to 15 kg each, one cabin bag and a couple of backpacks. Tell us what you are carrying when you book on WhatsApp and we send the car that fits it.

Can four adults travel with four large suitcases in one cab?

Yes, in the BYD E6. It is a full size electric MPV and it takes four large suitcases along with two cabin suitcases and backpacks while still sitting up to 4 guests. The Tata Punch EV will not do this and we will not pretend otherwise. Ask for the BYD when you book if you are carrying a full set of checked bags.

Do both Aavre cars seat the same number of people?

Yes. Every Aavre cab sits up to 4 guests, in either model. Seats are never the thing that changes between our two cars. Luggage room is.

We are five or six people travelling together. What happens?

That is two cars, and we will tell you so when you book rather than squeezing you into one. Both are pre confirmed and both arrive together, so the group travels as one. Message us with your group size and luggage and we will quote the pair before you travel.

Can I choose which car comes?

Yes. Name the BYD E6 or the Tata Punch EV when you book on WhatsApp, or simply tell us what you are carrying and let us choose. There is no charge for asking, and we would rather send the bigger car than have you travel with a suitcase on your knees.

Which car is better for a Kumbhalgarh or Chittorgarh day trip?

The BYD E6, if you have the choice. Both cars complete the round trip comfortably, but these are long days of over 200 kilometres with climbs in them, and the extra space matters more at hour eight than it does at hour one. For city sightseeing, where the car stops every forty minutes, the Tata Punch EV is the easier car through the old city lanes.
