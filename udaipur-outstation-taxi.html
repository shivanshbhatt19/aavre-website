Common questions

Do you offer outstation taxi service from Udaipur?

Yes. Nathdwara, Kumbhalgarh, Chittorgarh and Ranakpur are fixed fare routes. For any other destination, such as Mount Abu or Ahmedabad, we quote a fixed price on WhatsApp before you confirm.

Do you offer one way taxi from Udaipur?

Yes. One way transfers to Nathdwara are ₹1,200, Kumbhalgarh and Chittorgarh ₹2,000, and Ranakpur ₹2,200. Other one way trips are quoted to your route.

How do I get a quote for a custom route?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94143 43817 with your pickup, destination, date, guests and bags, and whether you need a one way or round trip. We reply with a fixed price.

Are toll and parking included in outstation fares?

No. Toll and parking are charged at actuals on every route.

What happens if we stay longer at the destination?

Outstation transfers include 30 minutes of free waiting. After that, the trip converts to a full day hire at ₹2,500, and the difference is settled at trip end.
