Which taxi apps work in Udaipur?

Uber, Ola and Rapido all operate in Udaipur. Whether a car actually turns up when you need it is another matter. Here is what to expect, and why most visitors pre book.

The reliable way to travel

If you are used to booking a cab in seconds in Delhi, Mumbai or Bengaluru, Udaipur can be a surprise. The apps are here and they do work. There are simply far fewer cars on them, and the gaps show up exactly when you need a ride most.

Can I use Uber in Udaipur?

Yes, Uber operates in Udaipur. Within the city on a weekday afternoon you will often find a car. It gets unreliable at the edges: very early mornings, late nights, at the airport, on long trips and during peak tourist weeks, when waits get longer, cancellations more common and fares climb with demand.

Is Ola available in Udaipur?

Yes, Ola is available in Udaipur too, along with Rapido. The same pattern applies. Fine for a short hop when cars are around, less dependable when you have a flight to catch or a day planned.

Where app cabs fall short in Udaipur

  • Airport arrivals and departures. A small airport 22 km from the city means few app cars waiting nearby, and a long wait if none accepts.
  • Early mornings and late nights. Fewer drivers are online, so a 5 am airport run is a gamble.
  • Peak season. From October to March demand jumps, and fares and waiting times jump with it.
  • Cancellations. Drivers can cancel after seeing the drop point or the distance, often after you have waited.
  • Day trips. Apps are built for one way city rides, not a day at Kumbhalgarh with the car waiting for you.
  • Luggage. You get whichever car accepts, whether or not your suitcases fit.
