A full day of Udaipur sightseeing

A route that works for most first visits. Your driver adjusts it around opening times, crowds and the light.

01

Morning: the old city

City Palace and Jagdish Temple, before the day gets hot. Your driver drops you close and waits nearby.

02

Late morning: gardens and lakes

Saheliyon ki Bari and a drive along Fateh Sagar Lake.

03

Afternoon: haveli and lunch

Lunch in the old city, then Bagore ki Haveli by Gangaur Ghat.

04

Evening: views and sunset

Up to Sajjangarh, the Monsoon Palace, or a boat on Lake Pichola for sunset.

Common questions

How much does a taxi for Udaipur sightseeing cost?

₹1,500 for a half day of 5 hours or 100 km, and ₹2,500 for a full day of 10 hours or 180 km. Extra km are ₹10 and extra hours are ₹100. Toll and parking are at actuals.

Can I hire a cab in Udaipur for a full day?

Yes. A full day hire gives you one car and one driver for 10 hours or 180 km, for ₹2,500. Use it for city sightseeing or a day out to Kumbhalgarh, Ranakpur, Nathdwara or Chittorgarh.

Is a half day enough to see Udaipur?

A half day covers two or three of the main sights, for example City Palace, Jagdish Temple and Lake Pichola. To see the city at an easy pace with lunch and sunset, a full day works better.

Will the driver wait while we visit each place?

Yes. It is your car for the day. The driver drops you close to each sight, waits, and picks you up when you are ready.

Can we change the plan during the day?

Yes. Add a stop, skip one or stay longer. Only time and km beyond the included limits are charged extra, at ₹100 per hour and ₹10 per km.
