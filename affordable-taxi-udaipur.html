When people search for the cheapest taxi in Udaipur, what most of them want is simple: a fair price, and no surprises. That is what we built Aavre around.

Why a low quote often ends up costing more

  • Surge and peak pricing. App fares move with demand, and a quote at noon is not the fare at midnight or in December.
  • Night charges. Many operators add a surcharge for early morning and late night rides, often mentioned only at the end.
  • Haggling at the kerb. At the airport or a hotel gate, the fare depends on the hour, the season and how tired you look.
  • Extras on the day. Waiting time, a second stop, a slightly longer route. Small charges that were never discussed.
