Udaipur local sightseeing taxi fare

Most of the city sights sit within a few kilometres of each other, so a city day rarely gets near the km limit. What you are paying for is time.

  • Half day, ₹1,500. Enough for City Palace, Jagdish Temple and a sunset at Lake Pichola, or Saheliyon ki Bari, Fateh Sagar and Sajjangarh.
  • Full day, ₹2,500. The whole city at an easy pace, with time for lunch and a long stop at the palace.

Common questions

What is the taxi price in Udaipur?

With Aavre, an airport transfer anywhere in Udaipur city is ₹499, a half day is ₹1,500 and a full day is ₹2,500. One way transfers are ₹1,200 to Nathdwara, ₹2,000 to Kumbhalgarh or Chittorgarh and ₹2,200 to Ranakpur. Toll and parking are at actuals.

How much is a full day taxi in Udaipur?

₹2,500 for 10 hours or 180 km. Beyond that it is ₹10 per km and ₹100 per hour, settled at the end of the trip.

What is the Udaipur local sightseeing taxi fare?

₹1,500 for a half day of 5 hours or 100 km, or ₹2,500 for a full day of 10 hours or 180 km. City sightseeing rarely comes near the km limit.

Do taxi fares in Udaipur go up at night or in peak season?

Ours do not. Aavre fares are the same at any hour and any time of year, with no night surcharge and no seasonal surge.

Are toll and parking included?

No. Toll and parking are charged at actuals, so you pay exactly what the toll booth or parking lot charges.

What if my trip runs longer than planned?

A half day that runs past 5 hours converts to a full day at trip end. On a full day, extra km are ₹10 and extra hours are ₹100, settled at the end of the trip. On outstation transfers, 30 minutes of waiting is free, after which the trip converts to a full day.
